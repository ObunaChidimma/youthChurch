Who we are

We are the young people of Grace Community Church, united by faith in Jesus Christ and a desire to live it out boldly.

Our fellowship exists to help every young person know God, grow in character and serve others. Whether you have been in church all your life or are visiting for the first time, there is a place for you.

Our vision

A generation rooted in Christ and shining in every community it reaches.

Our purpose

To worship, disciple, and equip young people for a life of faith and service.

What we do

Every week and every season, there is something to help you grow and connect.

Bible study

Open, honest discussion of Scripture and how it speaks to real life.

Worship

Heartfelt music and praise led by our worship team and open to all.

Prayer

Weekly prayer sessions where we lift up each other, our church and our community.

Outreach

Visiting, giving and serving, so our faith shows up where people need it.

Hangouts

Game nights, outings and meals that turn new faces into good friends.

Conferences and retreats

Yearly getaways for rest, teaching and a fresh encounter with God.
