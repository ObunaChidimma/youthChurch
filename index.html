WELCOME TO THE TRIBE

Who We Are

We are St. Jude's Youth Fellowship, the young people of St. Jude's, Omole Phase 1, and we call ourselves The SJYF Tribe.

Whether you have been in church all your life or are visiting for the first time, there is a place for you in the Tribe.

NOVEMBER, OUR MONTH OF VICTORY

Prophetic Declaration for November

Happy New Month! Welcome to November, our month of Victory, in our year of Limitless Possibilities.

Anchor Scripture: Romans 8:37

“Nay, in all these things we are more than conquerors through Him that loved us.” — Romans 8:37

I rise this day and decree by the authority of the Word of God: November is my Month of Victory! I hear the sound of triumph over my life, and I step into it by faith. Heaven has spoken over me, and no power on earth or below can reverse it. I am not a victim; I am a victor. I am more than a conqueror through Christ who loved me.

I prophesy to every mountain standing before me: be removed and cast into the sea! Every chain of limitation breaks now. Every voice of delay, stagnation and defeat is silenced by the blood of Jesus. What held me down in past months will not follow me into this month. My season of struggle is over; my season of supernatural breakthrough has begun.

I decree that the hand of the Lord is upon me for good. Doors that no man can shut are opening before me. Divine helpers are rising for my sake. Favour is going ahead of me into every classroom, office, interview, business, and relationship. Where I was overlooked, I will be remembered. Where I was rejected, I will be celebrated.

I speak to my mind: be sound. I speak to my body: be strong. I speak to my spirit: burn with the fire of God. I will not fear, I will not falter, and I will not fail. The anointing that breaks every yoke rests upon me, and the grace for exploits flows through me.

I prophesy that my testimonies this month will be loud, many, and undeniable. Joy will be my portion, increase will be my experience, and all who know me will see the glory of God upon my life.

From the first day to the thirtieth day of November, I win, I rise, I shine, and I reign. Thus says the Lord concerning me, and so shall it be.
In the mighty name of Jesus Christ. Amen!
