WELCOME TO THE TRIBE

Who We Are

We are St. Jude's Youth Fellowship, the young people of St. Jude's, Omole Phase 1, and we call ourselves The SJYF Tribe.

Whether you have been in church all your life or are visiting for the first time, there is a place for you in the Tribe.

Our Motto

Raising a generation of young people that embodies genuine love for God.

Our Life Together

Worship, prayer, the Word, drama, music, service and real friendships.

OUR MONTH OF DIVINE CONNECTIONS AND FAVOUR

Prophetic Declaration for October

Happy New Month! Welcome to October, our month of Divine Connections and Favour, in our year of Limitless Possibilities.

Scripture: Proverbs 3:4 (KJV)

“So shall I find favour and good understanding in the sight of God and man.” — Proverbs 3:4 (KJV)

In the name of Jesus, I… (your full name)… step into October declaring that this is my month of divine connections and favour. In this year of limitless possibilities, no limit set by men, by my background, or by past failures shall hold me down. The favour of God goes before me and opens doors that no man can shut.

I decree and declare that I find favour and good understanding in the sight of God and man. Heaven smiles on me, and people are moved to help me. My name comes up for good in rooms I have not yet entered. Helpers of destiny locate me. I am connected to the right mentors, opportunities, and friendships, and the right places. The Holy Spirit cuts me off from every wrong association.

I receive wisdom beyond my years. In my studies, career, business, and ministry, I excel. Examinations, interviews, applications, and proposals turn in my favour. Where others are rejected, I am accepted, and where others struggle, I find grace.

This October, my gifts make room for me and bring me before great men. I will not be hidden or forgotten. Like Joseph, Daniel and Esther, I rise to places of influence and use them for God's glory. My spiritual fire burns brighter as I grow in the Word, in prayer and in love for God's house.

October is my month. I am favoured, connected, free and limitless. It is settled in heaven and established on earth, in the mighty name of Jesus I decree and declare. Amen!
